Inflation vs the policy rate

Monthly CPI inflation (teal) and the BI-Rate (orange), %, Aug 2023–Dec 2024. The rate was hiked to 6.25% in April 2024, then cut back to 6.00% in September as inflation fell under 2%.

Sources: BPS (CPI) and Bank Indonesia (BI-Rate), Aug 2023–Dec 2024.

Inflation month by month

CPI inflation, year-on-year, %, Aug 2023–Dec 2024. It peaked at 3.27% in August 2023 and ended 2024 at 1.57% — inside Bank Indonesia's 2.5% ± 1% target band.

Source: BPS, Aug 2023–Dec 2024.

Reserves at a record

Foreign-exchange reserves, US$ billion, end of year, 2020–2024. The 2024 figure, US$155.7bn, is the highest in the series.

Source: Bank Indonesia, 2020–2024.

The rupiah's year

Rupiah per US dollar, annual average, 2020–2024. Note: 15,855 is the annual average for 2024, not the end-year rate (which was 16,157).

Sources: Bank Indonesia; 2024 annual average: CIA World Factbook.

How to read this page: inflation is the change in the consumer price index from the same month a year earlier; the BI-Rate is Bank Indonesia's 7-day reverse repo rate, the benchmark for bank lending. Definitions →
