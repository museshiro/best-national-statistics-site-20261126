Indonesia at a glance

  • 17,500+ islands across three time zones, from Aceh to Papua — the world's largest archipelago.
  • 38 provinces, from Jakarta (10.7m people in 664 km²) to Highland Papua.
  • Southeast Asia's largest economy — GDP US$1.40 trillion nominal, US$4.67 trillion at purchasing-power parity (IMF estimate, 2024).
  • US$155.7 billion in foreign-exchange reserves at end-2024 (Bank Indonesia).
  • US$31.0 billion goods trade surplus in 2024 — exports US$264.7bn, imports US$233.7bn.

One number to remember: poverty fell to 8.57% in September 2024 — 24.06 million people, the lowest rate since records began in 1960. (March 2024 was 9.03%; the September figure is the one to quote.)
