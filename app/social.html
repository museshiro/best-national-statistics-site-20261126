Human development, 2015–2024

HDI (BPS scale, 0–100). It rose every year except the flat pandemic year of 2020, gaining 5.47 points in a decade.

Source: BPS, 2015–2024.

Poverty, 2015–2024

Poverty rate, %, September each year. The pandemic pushed it back over 10% in 2020; by September 2024 it was 8.57% — the lowest since 1960.

Source: BPS, September each year. Poverty line Sep 2024: Rp595,242 per person per month.

Inequality, 2015–2024

Gini ratio, March each year (0 = perfect equality, 1 = one person holds everything). It fell from 0.408 in 2015 to 0.379 in 2024.

Source: BPS, March each year, 2015–2024.

Jobs, 2015–2024

Open unemployment rate, %, August each year. It spiked to 7.07% in 2020 and fell to 4.91% by August 2024 — the lowest in the series.

Source: BPS (Sakernas), August each year, 2015–2024.
