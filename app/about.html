About & sources

Indonesia in Numbers is an independent, original statistics explorer. It is a static snapshot: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript files that make no network requests and work offline, in a desktop browser and on a phone.

Sources

Every figure on this site comes from one of these publishers. The data vintage is 2024 (BPS releases of February 2025 and through 2025), except minimum wages, which are 2025.

How to use this site

  • Year filter (Dashboard, Economy): rebuilds the year-in-focus table for any year 2015–2024.
  • Tabs switch a chart between views — nominal vs real GDP, exports vs balance, island groups vs Java.
  • Click any table header to sort it; click again to reverse.
  • Search boxes filter the commodities, provinces and wages tables live, with a “showing N of M” count.
  • View as table under every chart shows the same data as an accessible table.
  • Hover any chart point or bar for its exact value.
  • Dark mode (top right) is remembered on your device.
  • Deep links work: try population.html#tab=islands:java or index.html#yr=2020.

Data dictionary

GDP (nominal)
Gross domestic product at current prices. 2024: Rp22,139 trillion (BPS). Per capita divides it by the mid-year population.
Real GDP growth
Year-on-year change in GDP at constant prices. 2024: 5.03% (BPS).
CPI inflation
Year-on-year change in the consumer price index. December 2024: 1.57% (BPS).
BI-Rate
Bank Indonesia's 7-day reverse repo rate, the policy benchmark. 6.00% at end-2024 (Bank Indonesia).
Open unemployment rate (TPT)
Unemployed people as a share of the labour force, August survey. 2024: 4.91% (BPS Sakernas).
Poverty rate
Share of people below the national poverty line (Rp595,242 per person per month, Sep 2024). September 2024: 8.57% (BPS). Note: March 2024 was 9.03% — the two are different surveys, not a revision.
Gini ratio
Inequality of expenditure, 0 (perfect equality) to 1. March 2024: 0.379 (BPS).
HDI (IPM)
Human Development Index on the BPS 0–100 scale (health, education, income). 2024: 75.02 (BPS).
GRDP
Gross Regional Domestic Product — GDP for one province. Jakarta 2024: ≈Rp3,400 trillion (BPS, rounded).
UMP
Provincial minimum wage (Upah Minimum Provinsi), set by governor decree. 2025 values; national average rise 6.5% (Permenaker No. 16/2024).
Foreign-exchange reserves
Bank Indonesia's reserve assets, end of year. Dec 2024: US$155.7 billion.

Verification notes

  • Poverty: the September 2024 rate is 8.57%; 9.03% is the March 2024 figure. An earlier draft mixed them up — this site uses September throughout.
  • Minimum wages 2025: East Java is Rp2,305,985 and West Nusa Tenggara Rp2,602,931 (corrected from earlier drafts). The value once listed for Highland Papua could not be verified against a decree, so it is not shown.
  • The 2024 rupiah figure, Rp15,855 per US dollar, is the annual average, not the end-year rate (16,157).
  • Provincial GRDP nominal values are rounded from BPS provincial tables and marked ≈; only Jakarta, Banten and Yogyakarta per-capita values are the published BPS figures.
  • Island population shares are rounded from BPS and sum to 100%.
  • Two charts were dropped rather than guessed: sector growth rates for 2024 and inflation by expenditure group, whose values could not be verified before publication.
